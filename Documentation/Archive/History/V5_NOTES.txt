BEAST SOCCER V5 — SMALL CORRECTION PASS

Changes from v4:
- Slightly easier tackles: range 0.98 -> 1.05.
- Expanded valid tackle angles by shrinking the protected rear cone from 60° half-angle to 50°.
- Small camera shake on every human and AI tackle attempt.

- Goal line test uses the ball centre crossing the line so enlarged/high-speed shots do not visually enter the net without counting.
- Menu/pause typography changed from very heavy display faces to Trebuchet/Verdana/Arial fallbacks.
- Normal menu text is regular weight with much lighter outline/shadow; PAUSED keeps only a modest bold hierarchy.
